Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FlagsRally app for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the App"), provided by Bacon.dev ("we", "us"), handles your information.

In short

  • No account is needed. Your records stay on your device.
  • We do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • The App shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking of our own.
  • Your location is used only while you use the App. When you record an arrival, its coordinates are sent to Google and TomTom to find the country and region.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your arrivals (the date, the coordinates and the country, region and place names), your check-ins on stamp trails, the stamp trails you add, and your settings, including your country of residence and, if you enter one, your Google Maps API key. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it.

If you use Create backup, the App makes a password-protected file of your records and lets you choose where to save or share it. That file is under your control.

2. Location

With your permission, the App uses your device's precise location only while you are using it:

  • to show where you are on the map;
  • to check that you are within 50 m of a stamp-trail place before a check-in;
  • to record an arrival. The coordinates of that place are then sent to Google's Geocoding API (with your own key if you entered one) and to TomTom's reverse geocoding API, which return the country, region and place names.

The App does not use your location in the background. You can change the permission at any time in your device's settings.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the services below. When it connects to them, they receive your IP address and the details of the request as part of normal internet communication, and handle them under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Maps Platform (maps and geocoding). The Google Maps SDK may collect usage and diagnostic data. Google Privacy Policy
  • TomTom (finding the region of a place). TomTom Privacy Notice
  • RevenueCat (managing the subscription). It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (payments). We do not receive your payment details.
  • Google Drive (the shared list of stamp trails is read from a public folder; no sign-in is needed).
  • Image and map data: flags from flagcdn.com, the passport cover from passportindex.org, map shapes from our content host, and images named in the stamp trails you add, from the sites that host them.

4. How long information is kept and how to delete it

Your records stay on your device until you delete them or the App. To delete subscription records held by RevenueCat, or for any other request, contact us at the address below.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date it takes effect.
