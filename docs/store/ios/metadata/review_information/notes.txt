No sign-in is needed.

Location: the app uses the location only while it is in use, to stamp a place when the user is within 50 m of it. To try it on a device away from the sample places, press and hold anywhere on the Map tab to put a pin there, then tap "Stamp here".

Stamp trails: Collections > Add stamp trail lists trails shared in a public Google Drive folder; tick one and add it. Its places then appear on the Map tab.

Subscription: a stamp needs the country and region of the place, which the app looks up by sending its latitude and longitude to Google's Geocoding API. The first 5 arrivals are free; recording a 6th arrival on the Map tab opens the subscription screen (monthly or yearly, auto-renewable). It can also be opened from Settings. The subscription screen shows each plan's length and price and links to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, which are also linked at the end of the App Store description and in Settings. Instead of subscribing, a user can enter their own Google Maps API key in Settings; the app then uses that key for the lookups and the limit does not apply.

Stamp designs: subscribers and users with their own API key can also choose how their passport stamps look in Settings > Passport stamps (several designs and a slight tilt). Everyone else gets the original design.

Advertising: the app contains no advertising. It shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK or ad network. Its third-party SDKs are Google Maps and Syncfusion Maps (map display) and RevenueCat (the subscription); the only in-app purchase is the subscription.
